# 07 — African Rainbow Capital (ARC)

**Type:** Black-owned investment holding / family-office scale | **HQ:** Johannesburg | **X:** ❌ none (⚠️ @AfricanRainbow1 is a UK charity — do NOT use) | **DoW fit:** ★★★ (indirect)

## Snapshot
Formed **2015**; fully black-owned and controlled; a 100% subsidiary of **Ubuntu-Botho Investments (UBI)**, which holds ~14% (economic) of Sanlam (c.R20bn) — making ARC a Sanlam-ecosystem powerhouse. Co-CEOs **Johan van der Merwe** and **Johan van Zyl** (ex-Sanlam group CEO); founder/backer network includes **Patrice Motsepe**. Listed vehicle ARCI is redomiciling from Mauritius back to South Africa (buyout announced 2025).

## Mandate & thesis
- Long-term stakes in **financial services** and diversified (non-financial) industries; seeks businesses delivering exceptional ROE.
- Reshaped portfolio: growing Fintech exposure, disposal of smaller non-core assets (Afrimat, Payprop, Humanstate, etc.).

## Portfolio (DoW-relevant signals)
- **rain** (telecom, 20.3%), **TymeBank** (digital bank; 25% sold to Sunlum 2025), Kropz (phosphates), Optasia (airtime credit), GoSolr (solar), ARCH Emerging Markets, Moloto Capital.
- **Bottom-of-pyramid / affordable-access theme runs through everything** — that's the DoW alignment: democratising access (banking→healthcare).

## Where to find them
- Website newsroom; CNBC Africa interviews; no X. Individual leaders occasionally quoted in press.
- Events: financial-services leadership forums, Sanlam ecosystem events.

## DoW pitch hook
Indirect: pitch "healthcare access at scale for the banked-non-doctored **TymeBank-style** mass market" — a coalition story (mobile health + digital banking rails). Expect hurdle: ARC does large-scale, capital-heavy deals, not startup seeds.
