# 02 — 54 Collective (formerly Founders Factory Africa)

**Type:** Venture Studio / VC | **HQ:** Johannesburg (offices Kenya, Nigeria, UK) | **X:** @54collective_vc ✅ (dormant) | **DoW fit:** ★★★ (legacy warm contacts)

## Snapshot
Dormant as of **July 2025**. The Mastercard Foundation terminated its partnership after a dispute; a South African High Court ordered provisional liquidation of its operating entity (Africa Founders Ventures, AFV). Its $40m UAF1 fund and certain vehicles were reported as still active at shutdown time — verify before engaging.

## What it was
- Launched in Johannesburg **2018** as Founders Factory Africa (UK Founders Factory spin-off), in partnership with Standard Bank; later Netcare (e-health, 2019–2021 goal of backing 35 e-health startups) and Small Foundation (agri-tech).
- **2023:** secured **US$114m** from Mastercard Foundation + Johnson & Johnson.
- **Aug 2024:** rebranded to 54 Collective.
- Said to have supported 70+ ventures, helped portfolio raise c.$140m follow-on; portfolio at closure c.40+ startups, ~17,500 jobs.
- CEO: Bongani Sithole.

## DoW relevance
- **Netcare e-health partnership** is the direct link: FFA built one of SA's biggest health-tech pipelines. Any of those founders/portfolio companies are warm referral paths into the health-tech community.
- Portfolio alumni to mine: BuuPass, Lipa Later, Zanifu, Shamba Pride, VunaPay, Asaak, etc.

## Where to find them now
- Defunct entity — do NOT pitch. Instead, surface **alumni founders** on LinkedIn and get *them* to champion DoW; some now advise/angel elsewhere.

## Contact routes
- Dead. LinkedIn search "ex-54 Collective" / "ex-Founders Factory" for warm intros.

## DoW pitch hook
Not targetable directly. Use as a **referral ecosystem play** (Netcare network + alumni).
